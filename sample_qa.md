# Sample Questions and Answers

This file contains example questions and answers from the Mutual Fund Facts Assistant.

All factual answers use official AMC, SEBI or AMFI sources.

## Q1
**Question:** What is the minimum SIP for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?

**Answer:** The minimum SIP amount for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund is ₹100.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/direct

---

## Q2
**Question:** What is the exit load for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?

**Answer:** An exit load of 1% applies if units are redeemed or switched out within 1 year from the date of allotment. No exit load applies after 1 year.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/direct

---

## Q3
**Question:** What is the benchmark of HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?

**Answer:** The benchmark of HDFC Flexi Cap Fund is the NIFTY 500 Total Returns Index.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/direct
